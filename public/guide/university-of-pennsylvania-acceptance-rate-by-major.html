University of Pennsylvania: acceptance rate by major

University of Pennsylvania admits about 5.4% of applicants overall. That number is the one everybody quotes, and for most applicants it is the wrong one — you are not admitted by the university, you are admitted by a college or programme inside it, and those rates differ sharply.

The short version: Business is the hardest route in at 4.5%. Nursing admits 8%.

What this means if you are applying from outside the US

Two things follow from the table above.

  • Your odds depend on what you write in the major box. At University of Pennsylvania the gap between the hardest and most accessible programme is 4.5% against 8% — a bigger difference than almost anything you can change about your profile this year.
  • International admit rates run below the published figure at most US universities. University of Pennsylvania charges international students about $63,452 in tuition, and its aid policy for them is: Need-based aid available.

Switching major purely for the admit rate is usually a mistake — a "why this major" essay written about a subject chosen for its odds reads exactly like one, and internal transfer after enrolment is capped and competitive at most schools. But if you were genuinely choosing between two subjects, the difference above is real and worth knowing.
